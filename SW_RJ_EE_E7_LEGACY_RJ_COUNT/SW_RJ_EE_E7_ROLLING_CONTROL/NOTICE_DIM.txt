SW_RJ_EE_PAIRWISE, prepared 2026-09-15.
Baseline: the user's SW_RJ_EE_TUNING, derived in this workspace from the
supplied v4 surface-wave project and DIM_MATLAB_Core(3).zip.
Original DIM repository: https://github.com/jeriedel/dim

DIM-inspired ingredients: fixed energy rings, uniform historical-time-entry
selection and full-history organization. This is an adaptation, not a full
DIM port or a paper figure reproduction. New pairwise pilot calibration and
optional sparse-ring merging are project-specific engineering choices.
Schreck ongoing adaptive quantiles and DIM BSP are not included.

Likelihood-only tempering preserves the existing unheated RJ prior.
Optional Route A depth proposals and the existing likelihood-floor branch
are included but disabled by default. The original inversion plotting
functions are preserved; EE diagnostic figures are updated separately.
See docs/BASELINE_MANIFEST.json and docs/CHANGES.patch for file differences.
